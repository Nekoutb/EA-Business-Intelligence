Built by accountants who run audits

The expertise is in the code, not the consulting hours.

Every BMS module encodes a specific piece of OHADA / IFRS / CGI knowledge. No-one on your team needs to remember it — the platform applies it automatically and consistently across every report. Audit-ready by default.

OHADA · CLASS 70
Revenue HT, not TTC
Revenue is always sourced from amount_untaxed_signed (HT) on customer invoices, matching the OHADA P&L definition and class 70 GL movements. VAT (class 4431) is kept separate by design. Budgets compare apples to apples.
OHADA · CLASSES 6/7/8
P&L accounts zero out at year-end
On the Trial Balance, classes 6, 7, 8 are recognised as nominal — they reset every fiscal year via close-out journals to class 13X. No more polluted opening balances on the Balance Générale, regardless of how clean the closing entries actually were.
CGI · ART. 117
Invoice compliance, line by line
Every customer invoice checked against Cameroon's Code Général des Impôts — TIN format, FNE block presence, sequential numbering, mandatory narrative fields. Compliance gaps flagged before submission, not during inspection.
REVENUE RECONCILIATION
Scan → Bill → Cash, three legs
The full revenue-recognition chain visualised as a Sankey: Scanned (expected) → Billed (invoice posted) → Collected (cash in). Both leakages — bill leak and AR — visible in one glance. Drill-down to the underlying invoices in one click.
PRICE × VOLUME × MIX
Variance decomposed correctly
Revenue gap split into volume effect (act_qty − bud_qty) × bud_price and price effect (act_price − bud_price) × act_qty. Standard finance pattern, encoded once, applied everywhere a budget meets actuals. Net always reconciles.
CCC · 13-MONTH TTM
Cash conversion, annualised correctly
DSO · DIO · DPO computed on a trailing-twelve-month basis, with partial-period annualisation when the data window is shorter (e.g. a new tenant in month 4 of operations). No artificial spikes, no missing data points — the chart renders the full 13-month strip.
CONFIGURABLE REVENUE MAPPING
Product revenue from GL, by description
Map each product to one or more class 706 sub-accounts via name-pattern matching. Catch-all accounts (e.g. "Services vendus dans la Région") can be redistributed across products on a configurable share. Diagnostic panel shows every assignment.
VAT · 19.25%
HT / TTC, never confused
Every figure carries its basis. AR balances, payments, customer invoices: TTC (what the customer pays). Revenue, P&L, budgets, Class 70 GL: HT (what the business earns). The platform doesn't let the two mix.
MULTI-TENANT · NATIVE
White-label from day one
Each tenant gets its own Odoo connection, brand, period filter, chart of accounts. Super admins manage modules & services per tenant from /admin/tenants/{id}. Zero data cross-contamination. Bilingual (EN/FR) UI throughout.
